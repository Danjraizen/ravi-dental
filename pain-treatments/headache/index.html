Headache

Expert headache treatment at Dr. Ravi's Dental and Orofacial Pain Clinic

Home/Pain Treatments/Headache

Not all headaches are migraines — and many headaches that feel like they're coming from your head are actually originating in your jaw, neck, or teeth. At Dr. Ravi's Dental and Orofacial Pain Clinic, we specialize in identifying and treating headache types that have an orofacial or musculoskeletal origin.

Types of Headaches We Treat

  • Cervicogenic Headaches: Referred pain from neck structures (cervical spine, muscles, nerves) that presents as one-sided head pain
  • Tension-Type Headaches: The most common type, often caused by muscle hyperactivity from clenching, stress, or poor posture
  • Referred Dental Headaches: Pain from infected upper teeth that mimics sinus or migraine headaches
  • TMJ-Related Headaches: Pain radiating from the temporomandibular joint to the temples, forehead, and back of head

The Orofacial-Headache Connection

The trigeminal nerve (cranial nerve V) carries sensation from your face, teeth, jaw, and temples. When this nerve is irritated by dental problems, TMJ inflammation, or muscle trigger points, it can trigger headache cascades that feel identical to primary headaches. Many patients we see have been treated with migraine medications for years without relief — because the true cause was dental.

Our Diagnostic Protocol

We perform a comprehensive orofacial examination including palpation of the temporalis, masseter, sternocleidomastoid, and trapezius muscles for trigger points. We assess your bite, jaw range of motion, and cervical posture. Digital imaging rules out dental pathology as a contributing factor.

Treatment Approaches

Occlusal Splints: Custom night guards that reduce clenching forces and allow fatigued muscles to recover.

Trigger Point Therapy: Targeted pressure and massage techniques to release muscle knots in the jaw, neck, and shoulders.

Posture Correction: Forward head posture increases neck muscle strain. We provide ergonomic guidance and exercises.

Coordinated Care: For complex cases, we work with physiotherapists, neurologists, and pain specialists to ensure comprehensive management.
