Invisible Aligners

Expert invisible aligners treatment at Dr. Ravi's Dental and Orofacial Pain Clinic

Home/Dental Services/Invisible Aligners

Invisible aligners are the modern alternative to metal braces — clear, removable trays that gradually shift your teeth into perfect alignment. They're virtually undetectable, comfortable, and fit seamlessly into your lifestyle.

How Aligners Work

We take digital scans of your teeth and create a series of custom aligners. Each set is worn for 1-2 weeks, applying gentle, controlled force to specific teeth. You remove them only for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing.

Advantages Over Braces

  • Nearly invisible — no metal brackets or wires
  • Removable — eat whatever you want
  • Comfortable — smooth plastic with no sharp edges
  • Hygienic — brush and floss normally
  • Fewer office visits — change trays at home
  • Predictable results — see your final smile before starting

Are Aligners Right for You?

Aligners work best for mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and bite issues. Severe cases may still require braces. We offer a free consultation to determine if you're a candidate.
